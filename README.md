## 🎬 What We Do

SlimKat Media is a creative media and storytelling platform focused on producing meaningful visual content, capturing real-life stories, and building community through film, media, and service-based offerings.

At its core, SlimKat Media blends **creative production**, **personal storytelling**, and **mission-driven initiatives** to create content that resonates and leaves a lasting impact.

---

## 🧩 Our Services

### 1. Documentary Services

We provide **family-focused documentary services**, helping individuals and families capture their stories in a cinematic and lasting format.

This includes:

- Personal and family documentaries
- Legacy storytelling projects
- Custom filmed experiences

---

### 2. Film Production

We produce and distribute original films and media projects.

Current work includes:

- **Farming Freedom** (currently in distribution)

Our films are directed and produced by **Kayla Turner Thomas**, whose work focuses on storytelling that connects with real people and real experiences.

#### Speaking Engagements

Speaking engagements are available featuring **Kayla Turner Thomas**, offering audiences a deeper look into the stories, themes, and impact behind the films.

These engagements are ideal for:

- Film screenings and panels
- Community events and discussions
- Educational and storytelling-focused programs

Each engagement is tailored to the audience and can include behind-the-scenes insights, creative process discussions, and conversations around the film’s message and impact.

---

### 3. Foundation Initiatives

SlimKat Media supports and works alongside the  
**James & Kayla Thomas Foundation (J&KTF)** — a 501(c)(3) organization.

The foundation will serve as the umbrella for future programs, including:

- Community initiatives
- Educational programs
- Media-driven outreach

More details on programs and initiatives will be added as they are developed.

## 🌐 The Platform

This repository contains the source code for the SlimKat Media web application.

The platform is being built to:

- Showcase media projects
- Present available services
- Allow users to connect, inquire, and book services
- Support foundation initiatives and outreach

Future features will include:

- Service inquiry and booking forms
- Subscription and communication features
- Integrated media and project showcases

---

## 🛠️ Technology

The application is built using a modern web stack focused on performance, scalability, and developer experience.

Core technologies include:

- Next.js (App Router)
- TypeScript
- Tailwind CSS

Deployment and infrastructure:

- Vercel for hosting and deployment
- CI/CD via Git-based workflows

Additional integrations (in progress):

- Email services (Resend)
- Form handling and submission workflows
- Future database integration
